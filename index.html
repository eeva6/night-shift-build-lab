Idea Check

Start here

Idea Check is a DIY coaching tool we use alongside mentor feedback. Founders run it in week one and before each micro-grant milestone, then talk through the results with their mentors.

Spot the startup ideas hiding in work you know.

Start from your experience or from research about a problem. You'll list the problems you've seen, what they cost and why now, then get an idea card for each, with the ones worth testing first flagged and the investor themes they overlap.

Find the holes in your idea before an investor does.

Idea Check helps you pressure-test your idea, see where it's weak and decide what to do next. It checks your idea against Thiel's seven questions, the Lean Canvas, Techstars' team-first criteria, Y Combinator and a16z requests for startups, and 24 current VC theses, then gives you a test to run, adjacent markets to explore, and investors who back ideas like yours.

      The gap starts upstream.

      1.1%of US venture dollars went to all-women founding teams in 2025. That's the downstream gap.

      Upstream

      Too few women reach the stage of testing a scalable business.

      Night Shift

      Helps more women test early, then builds stronger paths to the next support.

      Read moreShow less

      Too few women get to the point of testing a business that could scale, and those who do still face barriers to capital. Night Shift focuses on that early stage.

      Nebraska context: 11% of women-owned firms have employees, against 22.5% of men-owned (SBA Office of Advocacy, 2025).

      1.1%: PitchBook-NVCA Venture Monitor, 2025 data. Guzman & Kacperczyk (Research Policy, 2019) linked about 65% of the funding disparity to growth orientation at founding, in California and Massachusetts registrations from 1995 to 2011.

      04 / 6 · How women-founded startups perform

      Women-founded startups do more with less.

      78¢ vs 31¢Revenue per dollar invested: startups founded or cofounded by women vs those founded by men (BCG and MassChallenge, 2018).

      63%How much better First Round Capital's investments with a female founder performed than its all-male teams, over 10 years (2015).

      Read moreShow less

      In BCG's MassChallenge data, women-founded startups raised $935,000 on average, less than half the $2.1 million raised by startups founded by men, yet earned 10% more revenue over five years ($730,000 vs. $662,000). In 2025, companies with at least one female founder were 25% of US venture-backed exits (PitchBook, March 2026).

      Nebraska has thousands of women like that: claims managers, nurses, engineers, ops leads who know exactly what's broken. They can't quit to find out if it's a business. Night Shift lets them test and build it first, without quitting their day (or night) job.

      BCG, 'Why Women-Owned Startups Are a Better Bet,' June 6, 2018; First Round Capital, 10 Year Project, 2015 (value growth from First Round's investment to exit or 2014 fair value, Uber excluded); PitchBook, 2025 US All In: Female Founders in the VC Ecosystem.
